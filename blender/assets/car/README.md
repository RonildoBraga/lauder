# car

A car as the greybox shows it: a body with a cabin of windows on top. The Brooklyn Bridge's cars are copies of it, and one, `car-b4`, is turned across the lanes and shortened to fill its 4 m box.

- **Source:** Made for Lauder with Claude's assistance on 19 November 2026, from the Brooklyn Bridge's greybox car parts, which the Lauder extension had made from the game's earlier drawing code. No third-party models or textures were used.
- **Licence:** The repository's [MIT licence](../../../LICENSE).
- **Size:** 2 m across, 4.6 m long and 1.5 m high; two parts, `car body` and `car cabin`, of 12 triangles each; two materials, `car paint` and `car glass`; no textures. About 90 KB, stored with Git LFS.
