# van

A van buried in snow, as the greybox shows it: a box with a band of windows, both white with snow, in the [visual direction](../../../docs/visual-direction.md)'s palette. The Brooklyn Bridge's `van-b` is a copy of it.

- **Source:** Made for Lauder with Claude's assistance on 19 November 2026, from the Brooklyn Bridge's greybox van parts, which the Lauder extension had made from the game's earlier drawing code. No third-party models or textures were used.
- **Licence:** The repository's [MIT licence](../../../LICENSE).
- **Size:** 2.2 m across, 5.5 m long and 2.2 m high, with the windows standing 1 cm out on each side; two parts, `van body` and `van windows`, of 12 triangles each; one material, `van snow`; no textures. About 90 KB, stored with Git LFS.
