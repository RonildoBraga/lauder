# Miki

The approved high-collar teal dress, swept hair and articulated hands, promoted from the [reviewed motion source](../../studies/miki-hand-motion/README.md). The game loads `apple/sources/content/models/miki.usdz`; this file owns the canonical surface, fitted 47-joint skeleton, four garment corrections and eleven local runtime Actions. Laura and Tom continue using the separate shared 17-joint party source.

- **Source:** The preserved Tripo P2.0 generation and original JPEG, with full [provenance and terms](../../studies/miki-option-a-tripo/README.md#provenance-and-licence). Original project skeleton fitting, finger skinning and motion are authored by Codex in Blender 5.3. The approved [hand rig](../../studies/miki-hand-rig/README.md) and [Run/Sneak/Shoot review](../../studies/miki-hand-motion/README.md) remain unchanged.
- **Licence:** Generated mesh/texture retain their recorded [Tripo terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms); they are not relabelled as an exclusively owned MIT asset. Original fitting, animation, tooling and project contributions use the repository's [MIT licence](../../../LICENSE), subject to the underlying recorded rights.
- **Size:** 10,269 vertices, 10,742 nondegenerate source faces and 20,305 triangles; one skinned mesh, one material and one unchanged 4,096² JPEG (1,665,979 bytes). Linear skinning matches the verified native path. Mesh, rest joints, skin weights, UVs, custom normals and garment targets retain the approved appearance. This exceeds the original stand-in budget and still needs physical-phone performance evidence.

The exact `miki` collection includes its local rig, mesh and required alignment parent. The original identity UV Mapping chain is expressed as the same active UV map, without changing UV coordinates or material values. The JPEG is unpacked byte-for-byte into `textures/miki-basecolour.jpg`, as the normal intake contract requires. Do not delete that parent or apply transforms casually: it supplies the mesh's geometry bind space.

`miki.<clip>` Actions cover idle, walk, jog, backward, both strafes, aim, accepted fire, hit, run and sneak. Run/Sneak retain the approved sampled poses; the other body motions are fitted from the original shared library. Measured wrist frames subtract the source precision-rifle grip origin and fit the existing carbine box: left wrist +4 mm laterally/−78 mm vertically, right wrist +12.5 mm laterally; the support hand slides 15 cm toward the grip for reachable arm extension. These are pose calibration offsets, with the reviewed wrist rotations retained; gameplay weapon geometry and muzzle are unchanged. Aim and the short fire reaction come from the reviewed shooting take; its editorial raise/delay is excluded from accepted-fire timing. The rifle remains separate and gameplay still owns movement, weapons, shot origins, accepted shots and damage.

Walk uses a baked two-segment leg fit to retain the original donor’s linear planted ankle travel at its declared 1.012931 m/s, rather than copying joint angles onto longer legs and accepting foot slide. Other body motion and source appearance stay unchanged.

The new strafe poses narrow lateral thigh excursion to fit the short dress while retaining forward flexion and the unchanged garment corrections; their source nominal speed is 1 m/s. Reviewed Run/Sneak remain unchanged at their 120 Hz export samples.

The named `miki47` profile validates every bone and parent, preserving the original 17-body hierarchy plus three joints per digit. Export concatenates the saved Actions, samples the four ordinary garment channels and generates profile-specific timing and measured wrist/finger metadata in `PartyModelClips.swift`. The `party17` profile remains distinct. Use the normal source-bound generator:

```sh
blender -b --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python blender/scripts/export-model.py -- miki
```

Do not edit the USDZ or generated Swift data. [build-model.py](build-model.py) reproduces the initial promotion into fresh scratch outputs using `--source`, `--shared-motion`, `--output` and `--report`. After editing the canonical source, use the ordinary export path; the study remains a historical review source rather than an automatic overwrite source.

This is imported-buffer reconstruction, not GPU vertex readback or phone-performance proof. Separate preservation checks retain the approved Run/Sneak samples within 0.87 micrometres.
