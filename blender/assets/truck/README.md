# truck

A truck as the greybox shows it: a box in rust with a dark band of windows, in the [visual direction](../../../docs/visual-direction.md)'s palette. The Brooklyn Bridge's `truck-d` is a copy of it.

- **Source:** Made for Lauder with Claude's assistance on 19 November 2026, from the Brooklyn Bridge's greybox truck parts, which the Lauder extension had made from the game's earlier drawing code. No third-party models or textures were used.
- **Licence:** The repository's [MIT licence](../../../LICENSE).
- **Size:** 2.5 m across, 8 m long and 3.2 m high, with the windows standing 1 cm out on each side; two parts, `truck body` and `truck windows`, of 12 triangles each; two materials, `truck paint` and `truck glass`; no textures. About 90 KB, stored with Git LFS.
