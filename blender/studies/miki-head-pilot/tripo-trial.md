## Visual observations

The actual 3D viewer was inspected from front, three-quarter and a freely rotated side/rear angle, with wireframe on and off and no generated texture. The skull has depth, the neck attaches to a real head volume, and large facial planes broadly reflect the input.

Prominent projecting eyebrow slabs, ball-like eye details and a blocky mouth/chin transition need correction before judging this as Miki. The input already has heavier brows and larger eyes than the approved art, so the trial does not isolate all identity drift to the generator. These observations are visual, not a native topology or deformation audit. The browser views do not use the [baseline's matched cameras](README.md) and do not complete the Blender comparison.

Screenshots of the result and upgrade screen remain private evidence because they include account UI and unrelated gallery assets. No screenshot enhancement was used.

Recheck billing interval, currency, tax and total before any authorised purchase. No subscription or annual commitment is authorised by this trial.

## Provenance before a public model import

This was a free-tier output. The [main Tripo terms](https://www.tripo3d.ai/terms), dated **13 November 2026**, describe free output under section 5.2.1 as public with a waiver or broad irrevocable public licence, excluding original uploaded inputs. Section 5.2.2(b) says a later subscription does not retroactively make free output paid content. Later paid edits can create new paid content while the original retains its status. The terms restrict competing AI uses and require any AI-origin identifiers mandated by applicable law to be retained.

Official summaries conflict: the [pricing document](https://www.tripo3d.ai/product-doc/getting-started/pricing-credits) describes free commercial use with attribution, while the [commercial-use help](https://www.tripo3d.ai/help/privacy-policy/how-to-use-tripo-models-commercially) says free commercial rights are not granted and refers to the main terms. Preserve these dated distinctions and any trial-specific conditions before publishing an exported asset. Do not automatically mark the raw mesh MIT or claim exclusive ownership. No Tripo file is committed.
