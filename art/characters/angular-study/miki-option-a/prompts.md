Codex used the built-in image-generation tool. These are original project concept/reference illustrations, not native Blender renders. No 3D model was generated in this step.

## Selected concept A

Inputs: the original [angular trio](../party-angular-v1.png) as the primary graphic style/identity reference (Miki only), and the [earlier full-body v2 input](../miki-fullbody-input/miki-three-quarter-v2.png) for hair, identity and palette only. Historical bag/belts/equipment are excluded explicitly. Only the selected A output is copied here; B remains an unselected local comparison.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a fresh ORIGINAL character concept illustration for Miki, the adult female explorer and sniper in Lauder, a quiet frozen futuristic survival game.
REFERENCE ROLES: Image 1 (party-angular-v1) is the primary graphic-art style and identity reference; use ONLY Miki, the teal figure on the left. Image 2 (miki-three-quarter-v2) is secondary reference for swept hair, human identity and teal palette ONLY. This is a new concept, not an edit or a replica of the rejected 3D mesh.
ART DIRECTION: angular editorial character illustration with beautifully composed large planar shapes, elongated tapered limbs, a relatively small but readable expressive face, strong silhouette and negative spaces, subtle asymmetry and believable clothing construction. Charming, confident, observant adult woman with a softer composed expression and natural restrained facial features, dark almond eyes, warm complexion. Distinctive short swept black hair built from a few clean broad angular locks, not spikes, not a helmet. Clear face and visible neck. Aim for compelling professional character concept art, not a generic low-poly mannequin, blocky Lego figure or plastic 3D render. Broad flat matte colour planes and controlled hard-edged tonal shading; no noisy arbitrary triangulation or photoreal texture.
SHARED COSTUME: one elegant simple teal insulated DRESS with an integrated HIGH COLLAR. Charcoal close-fitting leggings, fitted gloves and sturdy slim mid-calf boots; cold-weather practical. No dress waist belt or raised waist ring. No scarf, cape, separate shoulder armour, puffed sleeve blocks, shoulder straps, satchel, side bag, holster, backpack, harness, pouches or buckles. Empty hands; no rifle, gun, tablet or equipment anywhere. No exposed mechanical enhancements, neon, cyberpunk panels or fantasy ornaments. No exposed cleavage or bare legs.
COMPOSITION: portrait image, ONE full-body character only, head and boot soles fully visible, centred with generous margins, front three-quarter view. Relaxed open hands slightly clear of sides, natural easy standing stance with both boots on the floor, no foreshortening, no heroic weapon pose. Neutral warm light-grey background with a restrained grounded graphic shadow, even legible lighting. No scene, no inset figures, no text, no watermark.
KEEP ALL OF THESE CONSTRAINTS for the variation described next.
OPTION A — COMPACT TAILORED DRESS: Light, nimble tapered silhouette. A precise slim standing high collar opens slightly at the front and frames the face without surrounding or swallowing the jaw. Clean close shoulder line, continuous tapered long sleeves with fitted dark cuffs. The seamless tailored teal dress is compact and gently flares below the hips; its mostly even upper-thigh hem has a short architectural diagonal notch and side vents, exposing plenty of charcoal leg silhouette for running. No long dangling panel. Use 3-4 considered teal planes to convey the form, with one subtle off-centre construction seam. Same matte teal family as the reference but a fresh simpler garment. The appeal comes from elegant proportion, poised expression, sweeping hair, deliberate angles and silhouette rhythm. The whole outfit should be easy to understand as a single coherent 3D garment.
```
