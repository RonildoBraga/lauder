## Selected design

![Selected Miki concept A](concept-v1.png)

Keep the angular graphic style, swept dark hair, composed expression, elongated tapered proportions and teal/charcoal palette. The outfit is one continuous fitted dress with a compact integrated high collar, small pale inner facing, tapered long sleeves, short notched hem and modest side vents. Tailoring shapes the waist; there is no belt or raised waist ring. No bag, straps, scarf, backpack or long trailing panel returns. Rifles and the field slate remain separate equipment and are absent from these body references.

This supersedes the rejected [full-body fit](../../../../blender/studies/miki-fullbody-fit/README.md) as the next visual target. The original [angular trio](../README.md), older modelling references, generated imports and Blender studies remain preserved as history. The new illustrations do not establish a biography, change Miki's explorer/sniper role or replace a canonical game asset.

## Next gate and lessons

Review the references against the selected concept, then generate one unrigged full-body candidate. Inspect actual front/side/back and clay/colour views in Blender before rigging. Keep the untouched raw generation and compare any simplification directly against it. The low-poly aesthetic does not require discarding the face or silhouette to reach an arbitrary tiny polygon count. Tripo's Smart Mesh route is a trial, not a guarantee of deformation-ready topology or appearance.

Fit the skeleton to accepted body proportions and use the existing shared motion as a retargeting reference. Test one run and sneak cycle before expanding. The rejected outfit's hundreds of garment corrective samples are not a reusable wardrobe solution for this design. If the new mesh needs major construction repair again, consider a focused character-artist topology/weighting pass instead of extending the patch chain.

No Tripo generation or credits, 3D edits, rigging, runtime integration, app checks, phone testing, merge or release are included in this reference step. Existing Blender files and the open preview are unchanged.
