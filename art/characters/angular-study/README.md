## What changes

The reference language is **angular graphic caricature**: small simplified heads, deliberately exaggerated body masses, long tapered limbs, strong negative spaces, large flat colour planes and hard graphic shadows. The design changes proportions and costume construction rather than adding accessories to the previous rounded figure.

| Character | Shape and attitude | World connection |
|---|---|---|
| Miki | Tall tapered coat wedge, small angular head, off-centre collar, unequal coat tails and thoughtful tilted posture | Teal insulated workwear, compact diagnostic tool and useful pouch |
| Laura | Lean arrow/diagonal, short jacket, long clear legs and an alert balanced stance | Orange route-worker jacket, high compact pack and narrow practical boots |
| Tom | Broad compact inverted trapezoid, small head nested in a strong collar, substantial forearms and shorter sturdy legs | Blue insulated load-handling coat and broad cargo harness |

The cast remains original human survivors in the frozen futuristic setting. Clothing covers them for cold, weapons stay secondary, and no external cybernetic anatomy is introduced. Colours retain their established roles. Apparent ages, faces, body ratios, costume details and carried props remain visual proposals; the [story bible](../../../docs/story/story-bible.md) still owns biographies and enhancement histories.

## Translation to 3D

This is a 2D direction sheet, not a game screenshot or an exported model. The assigned [static Blender pass](../../../blender/studies/miki-angular/README.md) attempted to translate its large planes and silhouette rhythm, but visual review found that earlier blockout needed reconstruction from approved modelling references. Random triangulation, bevels on every edge, glossy surfaces and large white eyes would weaken this direction.

The static blockout is separate from the earlier body and has no armature or skin weights. Its independent [character source](../../../blender/studies/miki-angular/character/miki-angular-character.blend) owns four body/clothing/holster/sling pose collections; each rifle and the field slate has a separate equipment source. The ten-scene review composition links them and owns attachment transforms, preserving the poses and cameras. This is authoring organisation, not runtime attachment implementation or proof that every rifle fits. Its review scenes distinguish the concept comparison, editable source components, exact phone-scale camera B and close angle-matched high rear framing. The proportions must not be forced to fit the earlier body unchanged. Reusing the skeleton hierarchy may be possible, but rest joints, skin weights, hand/weapon fit, foot contact and the shared motions require fresh verification after a design is selected. No compatibility, triangle count, material budget or phone-performance result can be inferred from the PNG. The smaller rear views are illustrative, not exact camera B projections or proof of phone readability. Engine, runtime rendering and the frozen bridge remain unchanged.
