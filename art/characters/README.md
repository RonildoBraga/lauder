## Earlier rounded-proportion concept

The remaining sections describe the first image, generated on 24 November 2026, and its original provenance. It led to the Miki mesh experiments before the angular-style request.

The [story bible](../../docs/story/story-bible.md) supplies their roles; the [visual direction](../../docs/visual-direction.md) supplies the frozen low-poly world, larger stylised heads and existing party colours. The trio meets as strangers. This image does not establish relationships, ages, ancestry, biographies or enhancement histories.

![Miki, Laura and Tom with distinct winter silhouettes, shown from the front and a higher rear angle](party-lineup-v1.png)

| Character | Proposed appearance | Intended reading |
|---|---|---|
| Miki | Teal tapered mid-thigh work parka, angular dark bob, pale collar and compact tool pouch | Careful technician and salvage specialist |
| Laura | Short orange winter jacket, close cap, tied-back hair, narrow outline and compact high backpack | Agile route reader and scout |
| Tom | Broad square blue coat, short hair and beard, substantial gloves and cargo pack | Controlled strength and load handling |

Each outline should remain distinguishable without colour. Faces, skin tones, apparent ages, clothing cuts, equipment and body proportions are exploratory choices, not newly agreed story foundations. Weapons in the image suggest the existing carbine, light burst weapon and scatter weapon; their illustrated mounting and geometry do not redesign the runtime weapons.

The large views explore identity; the smaller high rear views explore the shapes seen during travel. They are illustrative, not exact camera B projections, metric specifications, meshes or evidence of phone readability/performance. Broad shape changes, especially Tom's shoulders, need skin-weight, animation and weapon-clearance review against the existing shared 17-bone rig after a direction is selected. Runtime geometry must fit an agreed, measured character budget without relying on tiny surface details. Miki's higher-detail Blender study proposes a different appearance and budget for review; it does not change the current runtime target.
