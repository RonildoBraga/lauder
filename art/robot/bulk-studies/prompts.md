# Bulkier robot concept prompts

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 23 November 2026. These are three separate proportion studies after the maintainer rejected the first Blender blockout. No option had been selected at generation time. The maintainer subsequently chose C for a Blender proportion study and retained A for a possible later different-weapon variant; see the [current selection](README.md). The prompts below are the original generation inputs, unchanged.

Each call uses the common prompt below followed by its option-specific prompt, verbatim. Both input images are visual references, not edit targets:

1. [Selected dark armed robot concept](../robot-design-v3.png): identity, four-leg construction, twin gun pods, dark matte palette and square sensor. Its rznn7 reference chain and CC BY 4.0 notice remain in [attribution.md](../attribution.md).
2. [Bridge gameplay concept](../../bridge/bridge-gameplay-v1.png): sparse low-poly art direction, snow, lighting and simple colour planes. It does not set metric scale or exact camera projection.

The rejected Blender preview is deliberately not an input. All three calls request an opaque background.

## Common prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: mobile-game robot enemy concept art for visual review before any further 3D modelling.
Primary request: create a NEW much bulkier four-legged armed robot design, using the attached robot concept only as an identity/style reference and the bridge image only as an art-direction reference. The previous modelling attempt was too spindly and boxy. Change the mass distribution and silhouette decisively: a dense integrated body, substantial hip/shoulder housings, thick sculpted upper legs, stout articulated lower legs, compact planted feet and convincing weight. Bulk must come from proportions and connected armour volumes, not simply making the robot a giant boss or enlarging its guns. Do not reproduce a plain square box on thin rods.
Keep exactly FOUR legs total: two front and two rear; two compact side gun pods with ONE forward barrel each; a burgundy front mask with a single pale square sensor; two short dark antennae. No humanoid arms, extra legs, tracks, wheels, tail, extra weapons or new abilities. The sensor is flat pale paint/glass contrast, not an emissive light.
Style: restrained, attractive, very low-poly game concept; bold bevelled or faceted volumes, crisp silhouettes, a few broad armour plates, matte untextured colour planes. Use a small number of large forms with very few seams. No realistic metal textures, scratches, micro-greebles, cables, tiny screws or photorealistic rendering. It should be feasible to model faithfully for an iPhone game after a design is selected; do not sacrifice good proportions to an arbitrary triangle count.
Palette: charcoal armour, slate-grey lit facets, near-black joints and muzzles, restrained burgundy face, warm pale square sensor. Pale snowy ground, long blue-grey contact shadows and soft warm daylight. Match the quiet sparse world in the bridge reference.
Composition: landscape concept sheet. One large, fully visible front three-quarter hero view occupies the left two-thirds, camera about 30 degrees down. On the right, a smaller high three-quarter view of THE SAME robot, about 52 degrees down, beside one simple small teal-clothed human scale figure. The small view illustrates readability at gameplay distance; it is a concept thumbnail, not an actual game screenshot. Keep both views internally consistent and at a credible similar overall enemy size across options. Neutral weight-bearing stance, generous clear background, no cropped feet or antennae. Background consists only of a pale snow plane; do not redraw the bridge or add scene props.
Text: only the single option letter specified below, small and unobtrusive in the upper left. No other text, labels, logos, watermark, UI, aiming lines, warning cones, muzzle flashes, projectiles, combat effects or smoke.
```

## A — Armoured crawler

```text
Option letter: "A".
Design direction: ARMOURED CRAWLER. A broad, medium-low, compact crab-like body with a powerful shoulder collar wrapping around the front mask. A chunky chest and belly form one coherent armoured mass; the face is nested into a sloped bevelled front rather than perched as a separate cube. Four strong bent legs splay moderately outward, with broad tapered upper armour plates, substantial knee joints, shorter stout shins and compact flat feet. The two gun pods nest into the broad shoulder armour, small relative to the body. Make it look dense, grounded, stubborn and capable of carrying its own weight. Preserve clear gaps between the legs so the silhouette remains legible. This is the balanced bulky option: neither very tall nor pancake-flat, with mass concentrated in the body and upper legs.
```

## B — Low siege bot

```text
Option letter: "B".
Design direction: LOW SIEGE BOT. The most squat and broad-bodied option: a faceted beetle-like armoured carapace slopes over a compact core, with a strong low wedge-shaped roof silhouette. The square pale sensor and burgundy mask are recessed into the forward sloping face; retain visibility from the high camera. Four heavily built folded legs tuck closer to the hull, with thick angular upper sections, chunky exposed knee blocks and short sturdy lower sections. The two modest gun pods are integrated into the left and right hull shoulders, not large protruding cannons. Give it a heavy, stable, planted feel while retaining articulated walking legs and a clear ground gap beneath the hull. It must feel like a compact walking armoured vehicle, not a tank with tracks and not a thin spider under a helmet.
```

## C — Heavy walker

```text
Option letter: "C".
Design direction: HEAVY WALKER. A taller, forward-leaning industrial quadruped with a broad upright armoured torso, substantial chest and a thick waist/hip frame. Its shoulders and hip housings visibly connect the four weight-bearing articulated legs to the torso. Large sloping upper-leg plates, strong knee housings, stout shins and planted feet give it muscular mechanical weight. The burgundy face and pale square sensor form the front of a shaped wedge/chamfered torso, not a featureless cube. The compact twin gun pods sit securely at the sides of the chest. The stance is alert and slightly crouched, with enough clearance to move; body and legs should be much bulkier than the reference without making it a giant boss. Keep the overall footprint comparable to the other two concepts: the distinction is greater vertical body mass and a more powerful walking posture.
```
