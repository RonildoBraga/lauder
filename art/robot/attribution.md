## Source and changes

Based on **Low-poly Spider Bot (Rigged)** by **rznn7**, available from the [creator's asset page](https://rznn7.itch.io/low-poly-spider-bot), which identifies the original asset as [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The [public preview](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMjg0MTY2NS8xNjk3OTcxMi5wbmc=/794x1000/yEcRLJ.png) was used as a visual reference. No model file was downloaded or imported.

Lauder's AI-generated changes include a simplified four-leg interpretation, a darker matte palette, altered panel and sensor treatment, two added gun pods, body-pose studies and composition into the original [Lauder bridge concept](../bridge/README.md). The bridge background follows the project [MIT licence](../../LICENSE). No creator endorsement is implied.

Preserve this source credit, licence link and indication of changes when sharing the reference-derived PNGs. The original creator's asset is distinct from these AI-generated adaptations. Exact generation prompts and input roles are in [prompts-v3.md](prompts-v3.md).

The later [bulkier studies](bulk-studies/README.md) use the v3 sheet for identity and the bridge concept for art direction. Their additional changes redistribute mass into larger connected bodies, hip housings and thick articulated legs, with three different silhouettes. Their [exact prompts](bulk-studies/prompts.md) are preserved separately. The maintainer subsequently selected C for a new Blender form study and retained A for a possible later different-weapon variant. The human figures and high-angle insets do not establish metric scale.

The [C heavy-walker model, authored motion and renders](../../blender/assets/spider-bot-heavy/README.md) retain the same source chain and CC BY 4.0 file-level notice, with newly authored geometry, walking, turning, bracing and recoil, and their own measurements and limitations.

The runtime [spider-bot-heavy.usdz](../../apple/sources/content/models/spider-bot-heavy.usdz) embeds the full [canonical credit](../../blender/assets/spider-bot-heavy/CREDIT.txt) as layer metadata. The same notice is exported as [bundled acknowledgements](../../apple/sources/content/acknowledgements.txt). These runtime files retain the source title, creator, source/licence links and Lauder's changes without changing the licences of code or unrelated assets.
