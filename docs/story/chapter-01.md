# Chapter One — A Place to Meet

**Game-script draft 0.3 — 16 November 2026 (Australia/Sydney)**

**Superseded narrative draft:** The [story bible, draft 0.4](story-bible.md) now records the ensemble direction and a revised Chapter One outline. This script retains the earlier family relationships, solo salvage trip and remote Lara for reference; those assumptions need rewriting. All three playable characters now travel together. Do not use the dialogue below to settle the reopened family ties.

## Scene 1 — What fits in the bags

**Interior. The family's old rooms in the Basin. Morning.**

A window has been repaired with a translucent sheet. Beyond it, several floors of the opposite building are open to the weather. A municipal display still glows below a military roadblock stripped of its gates.

Inside, the rooms are almost empty.

MIKI checks the weight of her bag. Tools, food, sealed water, warm clothing and a portable filter. She considers adding a heavy bench clamp, then sets it down.

In the corridor, her father TOMAS hands a delivery book and storage keys to a neighbour taking over his route.

**NEIGHBOUR:** And the east tank?

**TOMAS:** The level's written down. Check it before you promise anyone a full delivery.

The neighbour leaves. Tomas lifts his own bag.

**TOMAS:** Ready.

Miki looks at the household supply valve again.

**TOMAS:** You checked it.

**MIKI:** I know.

He waits while she takes her hand away.

Beside the door are Nina's height marks. Farther up, faded marks record Miki growing up in the same rooms.

Miki photographs them on her handset. Its network indicator remains dark; the camera still works.

She checks that a small photograph of her late husband is protected inside her bag. Then she plays Lara's last stored message.

**LARA, RECORDED:** We have the room. It's smaller than the drawing, but it stays dry. We'll listen at the evening check-in. Nina wants to know if you've packed her cup.

Tomas taps his bag.

**MIKI:** She knows you've been using it.

**TOMAS:** It was standing empty.

Miki takes a last look at the rooms.

**MIKI:** I thought you'd want more time.

**TOMAS:** We've done what we said we'd do.

He holds the door while she steps through. They leave on the day the family agreed.

**Story purpose:** Establish a planned move and an ordinary family bond within a devastated world. Miki's father has already made his own decision to go.

## Scene 2 — The road on the map

**Exterior. East Span. Late morning.**

A burnt vehicle lies below the approach. Its armour carries no readable insignia. The newer footpath curves around it.

The span is closed.

Fresh survey marks cross a crack in the deck. People are unloading a small cart on this side; a section beyond the barrier has dropped out of alignment.

Miki checks their route card.

**MIKI:** This was open when they went through.

**TOMAS:** Three weeks ago.

He asks a traveller where the cart came from and when the inspection happened. The closure is recent. No one is waiting for a repair crew.

The main northern road is passable, according to an arriving carrier, but adds two dry days.

Miki measures their remaining drinking water with her eye.

**MIKI:** We packed for the crossing.

**TOMAS:** Then we don't take two extra days on a guess.

They have a contingency: Relay House, within reach on this side.

Miki marks the closure on their paper map. Tomas asks the carrier whether anyone has come from Old Lock. The carrier knows someone at the shelter who has.

They turn toward it.

**Story purpose:** Put survival pressure on an existing plan. The family adapts before an unsafe crossing becomes another dramatic accident.

## Scene 3 — Relay House

**Interior/exterior. An occupied relay shelter. Afternoon.**

The shelter occupies the protected ground floor of a former public communications building.

Blankets dry behind an armoured door reused as a windbreak. Drinking water is issued in measured amounts. A board lists meals, pump hours and names waiting for messages.

An unnamed KEEPER finds Miki and Tomas's destination in the handwritten contact book.

**KEEPER:** Your coast operator's on tonight's list. You'll be able to hear them.

**MIKI:** Hear them?

The keeper shows her the relay set. Its receiver works. When the transmit circuit draws power, the regulator cuts out. The fault has left the shelter unable to answer calls for several days.

Miki checks the markings against the maintenance sheet.

**MIKI:** This size was used in public transport emergency sets.

**TOMAS:** The depot had those.

**KEEPER:** The depot's been stripped.

**TOMAS:** Most of it. Let her see the drawing.

He points out a protected equipment bay in the old vehicle layout. He knows where it was, not whether anything inside has survived.

Miki studies the route. A short out-and-back trip is possible before dark.

Tomas will stay to prepare their household battery as a temporary supply for testing the repair. He will also speak with the traveller from Old Lock and compare reports from the two approaches.

**TOMAS:** I'll get you a road with a date on it.

**MIKI:** And a name. Who actually walked it.

He nods.

Miki leaves their travel bags under the keeper's care and takes only what she needs for the salvage trip. She agrees on a return time with Tomas.

**Story purpose:** Make shelter, supplies and reliable information matter. Miki and her father take different useful tasks toward the same goal.

## Scene 4 — What the fighting left

**Exterior. Transit service district. Later that afternoon.**

A civilian transit map has been painted over with a military diversion. Someone later scratched a walking route across both.

Miki follows the fresh route marks.

A public speaker starts halfway through an old announcement.

> ...remain indoors. Essential distribution will—

It stops. The entrance beneath it is full of fallen masonry.

At the depot, stripped interiors show how many people have already searched the place. Vehicle seats are gone. Wiring has been pulled from reachable cabinets. A cooking shelter occupies one intact bay; Miki leaves its connected equipment alone.

The vehicle in Tomas's drawing is trapped beneath a collapsed service gantry. Its emergency equipment bay is still fitted, though reaching it requires the narrower inspection passage.

A small security machine charges on a surviving solar rail across the service entrance. A military recognition unit has been attached to its civilian body.

It wakes as she approaches.

> ACCESS CLOSED. RETURN TO THE MARKED AREA.

Miki stops behind cover.

She observes its short patrol, checks the old maintenance plan and finds the inspection entrance outside its scan. She can pass through and isolate the access alarm.

A direct confrontation is possible, but dangerous and brief. Miki is trying to recover a part and get out.

Inside, she checks the damaged vehicle for anyone using it as shelter. It is empty. She confirms the regulator's markings against her copied sheet before working it free.

It takes time. A corroded fixing resists. Dust drops from the gantry overhead.

Miki stops pulling, supports the housing and changes her approach. The regulator comes free.

She inspects its connections and wraps it. Scrapes, dirt and effort matter more here than a pile of loot.

On the way out, an old service record catches her eye: the depot's grid connection failed before its first recorded military closure.

The terminal also warns that its clock lost synchronisation.

Miki notes both facts.

She does not yet know what either means outside this building.

**Story purpose:** Give salvage a specific purpose and cost. Establish the scale of violence through the place itself, while making the limits of historical evidence clear.

## Scene 5 — The things they know

**Interior. Relay House. Early evening.**

Miki returns before the agreed time. Her hands are dirty and one sleeve has torn.

Tomas has set water beside her place.

**TOMAS:** Sit down first.

**MIKI:** Show me the battery reading.

**TOMAS:** It's written beside the water.

She sits. He puts the reading in front of her.

The battery is usable. The regulator passes Miki's inspection. Together they prepare the supply while the keeper checks the antenna connection.

Tomas has fresh route information. A traveller reached Relay House from Old Lock by the service canal. There is a working water point along that path. The report is from this morning.

The coastward approach remains unconfirmed from their side.

Miki fits the regulator and tests the transmitter. The supply holds.

The keeper reconnects the set to the shelter's own power. It stays on. Tomas disconnects and repacks their household battery.

While they wait for the scheduled coastal call, she mentions the depot record.

**MIKI:** Their power was gone before they logged the military closure.

She turns the copied note toward him.

**MIKI:** The clock was failing too. It might not mean anything.

Tomas considers that.

**TOMAS:** Our lights went before the first warning.

**MIKI:** Do you remember how long before?

**TOMAS:** No. I remember what we were doing. Not the time.

They let the receiver speak.

**Story purpose:** Bring their separate work together. The family can build a practical plan without possessing the whole truth about the war.

## Scene 6 — A place to meet

**Interior. Relay House. Evening.**

The coastal operator reads the shelter's identifier at the agreed check-in.

Miki answers. For a moment the operator repeats the call, accustomed to receiving no reply. Then the shelter's name is acknowledged.

The keeper exchanges essential contact details and the East Span closure. Lara has registered for this appointment and is brought to the set.

**LARA:** Miki?

**MIKI:** We're at Relay House. Both of us.

**LARA:** You didn't take the north road?

**MIKI:** No. Not with the water we had.

Lara exhales.

**LARA:** Good. I was hoping you'd stop there.

Miki describes the crossing and the canal report. Lara supplies information from the other side: the coastal water crew is still using Old Lock. Its coastward approach has been checked.

**LARA:** Their boat goes up tomorrow. I can come with them and meet you at the crossing.

**TOMAS:** Someone walked from there this morning. We have a water stop on the canal path.

They settle on Old Lock the following afternoon. Lara will meet them with the water crew. Nina will remain at the coastal refuge with people she knows.

The operator records the plan. If a route is blocked, they will hold at the nearest staffed shelter and use the next radio appointment. They will not keep walking in the hope the other person has guessed correctly.

**MIKI:** Old Lock. Tomorrow afternoon.

**LARA:** I'll be there.

A smaller voice comes close to Lara's handset.

**NINA:** Is Grandad there?

Miki passes the set to Tomas.

**TOMAS:** I'm here.

**NINA:** Did you bring the cup?

**TOMAS:** In my bag.

**NINA:** You can use the other ones when you get here.

**TOMAS:** I'll have a look at them.

Nina asks for Miki again.

**NINA:** Mum, there's a place for your bag beside mine.

**MIKI:** Show me when I get there.

**NINA:** It's not a very big space.

**MIKI:** I didn't bring much.

Nina tells her about the room: the wind does not come through the wall. Miki lets her finish.

Tomas listens until the keeper signals that other families need the set.

Lara and Miki repeat the next call time. They finish with a practical detail: where the water crew will wait at the lock.

Miki writes it on the same map that now carries today's crossed-out route.

**Story purpose:** Deliver Miki and Tomas's live connection with Nina and a shared appointment with Lara. Miki, Lara and Tomas each supply something needed for the reunion.

## Scene 7 — Tomorrow's bags

**Interior. Relay House. Night.**

The repaired transmitter carries another family's answer.

Tomas checks the canal route once more, then folds the traveller's annotated sketch inside their map.

Miki refills their bottles within the shelter's allowance. They divide the next day's food and repack for the actual journey ahead.

The salvaged part stays in the community radio.

Tomas places Nina's cup inside his bag. Miki protects it with a clean piece of cloth.

Beyond the shelter, the old public speaker starts again and breaks off. Someone closes the windbreak.

Miki sets their map and the agreed call time within reach, takes off her boots and lies down.

They have water for the known route, somewhere to stop if it fails, and someone preparing to meet them.

**End of Chapter One.**

## Notes for the next script pass

- The six-year collapse timeline and the three-week household move are working choices, consistent with the proposed ages.
- The destruction establishes a large-scale war aftermath. Its causes, sequence and wider extent remain uncertain.
- The chapter begins with the family already acting on an agreed homecoming.
- Tomas is Miki's father and Nina's grandfather. Lara is a trusted friend who contributes current knowledge from the coast.
- Miki's husband, Nina's father, has died. His death is acknowledged quietly; its cause and timing are reserved for later development.
- Lara's background remains largely unknown to the player. Miki's existing trust in her is shown through their agreed plan and her reliable actions.
- The immediate achievement is a repaired transmitter and a fixed meeting plan.
- Before Chapter Two, map the canal route and Old Lock so travel, water and timing stay consistent.
