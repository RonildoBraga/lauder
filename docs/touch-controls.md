# Touch controls

The game can be played with the touch screen alone, for when no controller is at hand.

## Behaviour

- Without an extended controller, the screen shows a fixed bottom-left analog stick and **Look**, **Next: name** and **Pause/Resume** controls.
- With a controller attached, the controller is preferred unless the player selects **Use touch controls**; **Use controller** returns to it. The choice is kept in memory, not across launches.
- The keyboard and PS5/Xbox mappings of the [input contract](prototype-plan.md#input-and-interaction-contract) stay available. Touch **Look** requests the same intent as Cross/A/E: it shows the leader's action where there is one, such as **Search**, **Continue** in a conversation and **Restart** while paused.
- A **Fire** button beside the action button shows during a fight or once the carbine is drawn. It requests the same intent as R2/RT/F and follows the same rules: a fresh press draws, a hold keeps firing, and its finger must lift before the release gate clears.
- A **Signature** button left of **Next** shows during a fight in the combat yard ([ADR 0005](decisions/0005-squad-combat-experiment.md)) while Miki leads. It requests the same intent as Square/X on a controller and X on the keyboard: Miki's **Disrupt**, which cancels a shooter's wind-up within her reach and in her clear sight while the HUD shows **Miki can Disrupt**. A finger already on it keeps it shown until it lifts. Touch has no Previous button: **Next** cycles.
- A **Formation** button above **Next** shows once there are two companions. It requests the same intent as Triangle/Y/R and cycles **Close**, **Spread** and **Column**; the HUD names the formation after the party's names. While anyone is going or holding it reads **Follow** and calls everyone back. In a fight in the combat yard it reads **Regroup** ([ADR 0005](decisions/0005-squad-combat-experiment.md#after-the-second-session)) and brings the companions back to the formation. A finger already on it keeps it shown until it lifts, so the release gate still sees it let go.
- **Orders** ([ADR 0003](decisions/0003-squad-movement.md#orders), phase S3): once there are two companions, pressing and holding on open ground for about a third of a second opens a small menu at the finger. Releasing without sliding sends both companions to spots around the point; sliding left or right to a companion's name sends that companion, in the same left and right order the HUD shows for a controller; sliding down to **Hold** holds both where they stand. Sliding up, lifting before the menu opens, or moving more than about 12 points first gives no order. The marked point is what the finger's ray meets first, the ground or a block, fixed as the menu opens, so moving the leader with the stick meanwhile does not move it. A point nobody can reach shows a brief red cross, and a going companion's destination shows a ring in their colour. The corner around **Pause** and **Use controller** does not take the press, and the menu closes on Pause or in a conversation; its finger must still lift before the release gate clears, like any other.
- Touch has no button to hide the status panel, which always shows with touch controls; Circle / B or H does it with a controller or keyboard. Up to five buttons sit around the right thumb, the [proposed limit](gameplay-script.md#future-squad-and-combat-design), with **Pause/Resume** at the top; the fifth, **Signature**, shows only in a yard fight. Orders add no button.
- While paused, **Resume** stays at the top right and **Look** reads **Restart**. While the experiments last, a button left of **Resume** offers the pause menu's third choice, which names the next level in a cycle: **Combat test** on the bridge starts the combat yard, **Salvage test** there starts the salvage yard, and **Bridge** there returns to a new run of the opening.
- A stick finger works alongside button fingers. Lifting the stick finger stops; every contact must release before the neutral-input gate clears, even when the stick is centred.

## Input ownership

TouchInput supplies raw values to GameControls and the shared InputRules. Session mode, Pause priority, fresh action and switch edges and freezing the whole party use the same path as every other input; touch never bypasses the session.

Each control tracks its own finger. Source changes and inactivity cancel captures through generation tokens, so callbacks from an old capture cannot affect the new source. Pause keeps the capture surface mounted, so held contacts stay visible to the release gate. The screen stays awake while the game is in the foreground, Pause included.

Button presses are latched until the next sample, so quick taps are not lost between scene updates. Cancelling a source or a lifecycle change clears pending presses; InputRules still decides whether an intent is allowed.

## Checking

Touch play has been checked on the baseline iPhone 13 Pro Max: two-finger stick use alongside the buttons, one switch per press, stopping on release, Pause with held fingers, Resume after every finger lifts, and recovery after the app goes to the background. The order menu has been checked in the Simulator with a pointer but not yet on the phone, where its check needs a finger on the stick as well. The **Signature** button and the pause menu's third-choice button have so far been checked only by the unit tests of their touch input and input rules. Handing over between a physical controller and touch, and Xbox controllers, have not been tested on hardware. The Simulator, with a pointer, checks the interface but not physical controllers or multi-touch.
